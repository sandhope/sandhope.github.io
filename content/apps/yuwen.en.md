Ember — turn forgetting into visible light.

Memory isn't a saved file; it's light that dims. Every card has its own "memory temperature" that slowly drops over time. Review it once and it lights up again.

### Why Ember

Most vocabulary apps hand you the entire dictionary. Ember gives you only the few cards you actually need today. No leaderboards, no cartoon pets, no "3-hour streak" performance. Open, sprint, done — three minutes.

### Core experience

- **Memory temperature**: every card, wordbook, and your whole library has a 0–100 temperature, calculated by a spaced-repetition algorithm from each rating you give. Dimming isn't nagging — just a reminder that a memory is worth another look.
- **3-minute sprint**: 12 cards by default, due words first, with an estimate before you start.
- **One-handed gestures**: swipe left for "forgot", up for "vague", right for "remember".
- **Memory hooks**: expand a root-word hint when you're stuck; it folds away automatically.
- **Built-in wordbooks**: nine decks, about 8,800 high-frequency words — from beginner to GRE.
- **Works offline**: review on the subway or a plane. Your data stays on your device.
- **No account**: no sign-up, no ads. Data is stored locally and can be deleted anytime.

### Free & Pro

Core review and local data are free forever, with the beginner, CET-4, and gaokao decks included. Pro unlocks six advanced decks (zhongkao, CET-6, postgraduate exam, IELTS, TOEFL, GRE), memory hooks, unlimited decks, and focused review. Monthly, yearly, and lifetime options — no forced subscription.

### Privacy

Review history and settings are stored locally by default. No account, no personal data uploaded, no third-party ad SDKs.
