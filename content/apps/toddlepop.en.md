ToddlePop - first words that pop and talk.

#### Overview

ToddlePop is a first-words app for toddlers and preschoolers (ages 2–6). One item springs into the center of the screen, a clear English voice says its name, and the item waits for your child to tap, swipe, or move on. No menus to learn, no scores, no ads — just open the app and start noticing things together.

#### Core Features

- **Tap or swipe to browse**: An item springs into the center of the screen and a clear English voice says its name. Tap the item for a small animation and a sound effect — a siren, a moo, a drum hit. Tap the empty space or swipe for the next item, or swipe back to see the previous one.
- **240+ words in 8 categories**: On the Road, Land Animals, Planes, Trains & Boats, Ocean Animals, Birds, Nature & Plants, and Everyday Things & People — from fire trucks and excavators to penguins, sharks, fireworks, and drums.
- **Try it free**: 20 hand-picked cards on the home screen, with the same animations, sounds, and narration as the full library.
- **Parent controls**: Turn categories on or off, adjust volume and speech rate, choose an English voice, and set a screen-time reminder. American English is the default when available, with other English accents to choose from.
- **Screen-time reminder**: Off, 15, 20, or 30 minutes.
- **Made for small hands**: Big pictures, one clear action, and a parent gate (a simple math check) in front of every setting and purchase.
- **Fully offline**: All artwork and sound effects are stored on the device, and names are spoken by the device's built-in offline English voice. No download needed — good for car rides, flights, and anywhere without Wi-Fi.

#### Data Privacy and Security

- Designed for the App Store Kids Category and COPPA expectations.
- No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking, and no advertising or analytics SDKs. Your child's activity stays on the device.
- App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. See the [Privacy Policy](/toddlepop/privacy-policy).

#### Pricing

Free access includes the 20 try-it cards. All 8 categories require a subscription or a one-time purchase. ToddlePop Yearly is $19.99 per year and renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. ToddlePop Lifetime is a $29.99 one-time purchase with no renewal. No weekly or monthly plans, and no ads.

#### Is the app native?

Yes, it's native and written in Swift and SwiftUI.

#### Can you localize the app into my language?

ToddlePop teaches English words. The default voice is American English when available; parents can switch to other English accents in Settings, which sits behind the parent gate.
