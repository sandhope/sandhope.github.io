App-specific questions are listed on each app's own page.

### How do I report a problem?

Email me at [z502545@icloud.com](mailto:z502545@icloud.com). Please include which app you're using and your macOS/iOS version — that makes it much easier to help.

### Do your apps collect any data?

No. My apps don't collect any personal data or phone home.

### How do I get a refund?

Apps are sold through the App Store, so refunds are handled by Apple: [request a refund](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204084). If something isn't working, email me first — I may be able to fix it.

### Can I use an app on all my devices?

Yes. App Store purchases can be installed on up to 10 devices, including up to 5 computers. [Learn more](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204074).
