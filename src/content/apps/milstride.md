Milstride - Native Project Management for Mac

#### Overview

Milstride is a project management app built for the Mac. It keeps only what project management actually needs — projects, work items, boards, cycles, and modules — and makes it fast, quiet, and effortless. No account, no internet connection, no cloud. Open it and start working; everything stays on your Mac.

#### Core Features

- **Projects**: Run several at once, with custom identifiers (MIL-12) and colors.
- **Work items**: Title, notes, state, priority, start and due dates, estimates, and sub-items in one place.
- **Boards and lists**: Switch between List, Board, Table, Calendar, and Timeline at any time; drag cards to change state.
- **Custom states**: Backlog → Todo → In Progress → Done / Cancelled, with your own names and colors.
- **Cycles and modules**: Keep a steady rhythm with cycles, group themes into modules, and see progress at a glance.
- **Saved views**: Combine filters on labels, priority, and dates with grouping and sorting, then save the view you keep coming back to.
- **Insights**: Trends and distributions that show how work really flows and where it gets stuck.
- **Quick capture**: Jot down an item from the menu bar without leaving what you are doing.
- **Command palette**: ⌘K to search and jump to any work item, ⌘Z to undo, without lifting your hands off the keyboard.
- **Import and export**: CSV and JSON, so your data is always yours to take.
- **Local reminders**: Due and overdue alerts come from your own Mac, not from a server.
- **Drag and drop plus bulk editing**: Move cards across columns, reschedule in Calendar, and edit many items at once.

#### Made for the Mac

Milstride is written from scratch in SwiftUI and SwiftData, so it is a genuine macOS app: a three-column layout, native sidebar material, a collapsible inspector, system light and dark appearance, native shortcuts, and drag and drop throughout. No web wrapper, no browser in a window.

#### Data Privacy and Security

- Fully offline: no internet connection required, and nothing is ever uploaded.
- No accounts: no sign-up, no login, no cloud sync, no third-party SDKs or analytics.
- Your data stays yours: everything is stored locally and moves with your own backups.

#### Is the app native?

Yes, it's native and written in Swift and SwiftUI.

#### Is it really free?

Yes. Milstride is completely free — no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no ads, and no features locked behind a paywall. The full app, for everyone.
