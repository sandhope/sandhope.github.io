LivingSoil - A Living Desktop Ecosystem for Your Mac

#### Overview

LivingSoil is a desktop ecosystem made for macOS. Pick an animation in the app, and it quietly grows on your desktop beneath the wallpaper — plants, creatures, weather, and starfields that feel alive, without ever getting in your way. It is pure ambience: the layer ignores every mouse click, so your work stays undisturbed.

#### Core Features

- **Living Desktop Layer**: Animations bloom below your wallpaper, behind your icons; the layer ignores all mouse clicks so your work is never disturbed
- **Procedural Organisms**: Plants and creatures are generated from code (L-system growth + spring physics + noise), so every form is organic and unique
- **Browse and Cast**: A sidebar of categories, live preview cards, and an inspector let you pick and tune what appears on your desktop
- **Show Anywhere or Desktop-Only**: Pin animations above every window, or keep them resting beneath the wallpaper — your choice
- **Menu Bar Control**: A status-bar icon with a one-click "Stop desktop animation" escape hatch
- **Launch at Login**: Optionally restore your desktop ecosystem automatically on startup (SMAppService)
- **Personal Touches**: Import your own images or green-screen videos as creatures; adjust appearance, position, and themes
- **Light and Dark Appearance**: Follow the system appearance or choose a theme manually
- **Native and Efficient**: Built with Metal for smooth 60fps animation at very low CPU cost
- **Multilingual**: English and 简体中文, switchable in settings
- **Eco Tank Editor**: Arrange multiple creatures on your desktop, plant and grow them, and tune their forms with your own parameters to build a personalized ecosystem
- **Dance Zone**: Import your green-screen videos; GPU chroma-key composites dancing clips right onto your desktop at smooth 1080p 30fps

#### Data Privacy and Security

- LivingSoil works offline, with desktop content generated and rendered locally on your device
- No internet connection is required, and the app does not upload any imagery or data to the internet or third-party services
- The app does not collect, store, or share your usage content
- You remain in control of where macOS stores your files, including local or cloud locations you choose

#### Is the app native?

Yes, it's native and written in Swift and SwiftUI.
